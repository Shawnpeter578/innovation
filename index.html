01 — About us

What the
club is.

The Innovation Club is the School of Engineering's home for students who'd rather ship a working prototype than sit through another slide deck about innovation.

We run weekly build sessions, hackathon prep, and open project pods across hardware, apps, and applied machine learning — open to every branch and every year, from your first semester onward.

No club fee, no attendance sheet. You show up when you're building something, and you leave with people who'll debug it with you at 1am.

02 — Aim & vision

Why we exist.

A

Our aim

To give every engineering student, regardless of year or branch, a low-pressure space to turn ideas into working prototypes — and the mentorship to push those prototypes further than a classroom project ever would.

V

Our vision

A campus where building something real is the default extracurricular — where hackathon wins, published papers, and half-finished weekend builds all come from the same shared workshop culture.
